PREAMBLE: OUR PROMISE TO YOU
At Free-Fall, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right — not a legal checkbox. We are a student-led, independent news publication based in Hong Kong, and our readers include young people as young as 5 years old. That responsibility weighs heavily on us, and it shapes every decision we make about how we handle your personal information.
This Privacy Policy explains, in plain and honest language, exactly what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over it. We have worked hard to write this document in a way that is understandable not just to lawyers and adults, but to students, parents, and teachers as well.
We will never sell your data. We will never exploit your information for commercial gain. We will never share your personal details with advertisers. And we will always be transparent with you about what we do and why.
If you have any questions about this policy — whether you are 10 years old or 50 — please reach out to us at social.freefall@gmail.com. We are a real team of real people, and we will always do our best to answer you clearly and promptly.
Thank you for trusting Free-Fall with your readership.
— The Free-Fall Editorial Board, April 2026

SECTION 2: DEFINITIONS
For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, the following terms carry the meanings set out below:
"Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual — for example, your name, email address, IP address, or any combination of information that could be used to identify you specifically.
"Processing" means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, transfer, alteration, and deletion.
"Data Controller" means the organisation that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data — in this case, Free-Fall.
"Data Subject" means the individual to whom personal data relates — in this case, you, the user.
"Consent" means a freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of your agreement to the processing of your personal data for a stated purpose.
"Cookie" means a small text file placed on your device by a website, used to store information about your visit and preferences.
"Third Party" means any individual or organisation other than you and Free-Fall.
"Minor" means any individual under the age of 18, or the legal age of majority in their jurisdiction, whichever is higher.
"Newsletter" means Free-Fall's periodic email publication delivered to subscribers who have opted in to receive it.
"Platform" means the Free-Fall website, any associated applications, newsletters, and all digital channels through which we publish and distribute content.

SECTION 3: WHAT PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT
3.1 Free-Fall only collects personal data that is genuinely necessary for the operation of our platform and the delivery of our services. We do not collect data for the sake of collecting it. Below is a complete and transparent account of every category of personal data we may collect and how it reaches us.

3A: DATA YOU GIVE US DIRECTLY
This is information you actively and voluntarily provide to us when you choose to interact with certain features of our platform.
Newsletter Subscriptions: When you sign up to receive the Free-Fall newsletter, we collect your:
Email address
First name (optional, used to personalise your newsletter greeting)
School or institution name (optional, only if you choose to provide it)
Letters to the Editor and Reader Submissions: When you submit a letter, opinion piece, news tip, or other content to our editorial team, we may collect your:
Full name
Email address
School, institution, or professional affiliation (if you choose to provide it)
The content of your submission itself
Contacting Our Team: When you email us directly for any reason — to report an error, ask a question, or raise a concern — we collect your:
Name (as provided in your email)
Email address
The content and subject of your message
Tip Submissions: When you submit a confidential news tip through our secure tip line, we collect only the information you choose to share with us. We strongly advise against including unnecessary personal details in tip submissions. You may submit tips anonymously if you choose.

3B: DATA COLLECTED AUTOMATICALLY
When you visit the Free-Fall website, certain information is collected automatically by our servers and analytics systems. You do not need to do anything for this data to be collected — it happens as a standard part of how websites function.
This automatically collected data may include:
IP Address: A numerical label assigned to your internet connection that can indicate your approximate geographic location (typically at city or country level, not your exact address)
Browser Type and Version: For example, whether you are using Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or another browser
Device Type: Whether you are visiting on a desktop computer, laptop, tablet, or mobile phone
Operating System: For example, Windows, macOS, iOS, or Android
Pages Visited: Which articles and sections of our website you read
Time and Date of Visit: When you accessed our platform
Referring URL: The website or search engine that directed you to Free-Fall, if applicable
Session Duration: How long you spent on our platform during a single visit
Important: This automatically collected data is used in anonymised, aggregated form to help us understand how our platform is being used and to improve the quality and relevance of our content. It is not used to identify individual users personally and is not shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

3C: DATA COLLECTED THROUGH COOKIES
Free-Fall uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to improve your experience on our platform. We use the following categories of cookies:
Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are essential for the basic functioning of our website. They cannot be disabled without significantly affecting your ability to use the platform. They do not collect personal information and do not track your browsing activity for marketing purposes.
Analytics and Performance Cookies: These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our platform — for example, which articles are most read, how long users spend on pages, and which sections of the website are most popular. All data collected through these cookies is anonymised and aggregated. We use this information to improve our content and platform design.
Functional Cookies: These cookies remember your preferences and settings — for example, your preferred language or display settings — to provide you with a more personalised and convenient experience.
What We Do NOT Use: Free-Fall does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies for commercial profiling, or any cookies that monitor your browsing activity across other websites for marketing purposes.
For full details of the specific cookies we use, their purpose, and how long they remain on your device, please refer to our separate Cookie Policy available on our website.

SECTION 4: WHY WE COLLECT YOUR DATA — OUR LEGAL BASES AND PURPOSES
4.1 Free-Fall only processes your personal data when we have a lawful reason to do so. Below is a complete explanation of every purpose for which we use your personal data, and the legal basis on which we rely for each one.

Purpose 1: Delivering the Free-Fall Newsletter
What data: Email address, first name
Why: To send you the newsletter editions you have subscribed to receive
Legal basis: Your consent, given when you subscribed. You may withdraw this consent at any time by clicking "Unsubscribe" in any newsletter email or by contacting social.freefall@gmail.com
Purpose 2: Responding to Your Enquiries and Submissions
What data: Name, email address, content of your message
Why: To respond to questions, corrections, submission enquiries, or any other communication you initiate with our team
Legal basis: Legitimate interests — it is necessary for us to process your contact details in order to respond to you. We would not be able to maintain an effective, trustworthy publication without the ability to communicate with our readers
Purpose 3: Processing News Tips and Source Communications
What data: Only what you choose to share with us
Why: To investigate and potentially report on the information you have provided, in the public interest
Legal basis: Legitimate interests in the public interest — journalistic investigation and reporting on matters of public concern is a core function of a news publication and serves the wider public good
Purpose 4: Platform Analytics and Improvement
What data: Anonymised, aggregated browsing data, device information, IP address
Why: To understand how our platform is being used, which content is most valuable to our readers, and how we can improve the quality, accessibility, and design of our website
Legal basis: Legitimate interests — improving the quality and accessibility of our journalism is a core operational necessity, and the data used for this purpose is always anonymised and never used to identify individual users
Purpose 5: Legal Compliance and Safety
What data: Any personal data relevant to a specific legal obligation or safety concern
Why: To comply with applicable laws, court orders, regulatory requirements, or to protect the safety of our users, journalists, and the public
Legal basis: Legal obligation or vital interests, as applicable
Purpose 6: Ensuring Platform Security
What data: IP addresses, access logs
Why: To detect and prevent unauthorised access, malicious activity, cyberattacks, and other security threats to our platform and its users
Legal basis: Legitimate interests in maintaining the security and integrity of our platform

SECTION 5: HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR DATA
5.1 Free-Fall operates a strict data minimisation and retention policy. We keep personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. The following retention periods apply:

| Type of Data | Retention Period |
| --- | --- |
| Newsletter subscriber data | Until you unsubscribe, plus 90 days |
| Editorial correspondence | 2 years from the date of last contact |
| Published letters/submissions | Indefinitely, as part of our published archive |
| Unpublished submissions | 6 months, then securely deleted |
| News tips | 3 years, for legal and journalistic reference purposes |
| Anonymised analytics data | Up to 26 months, in line with standard analytics practice |
| Security and access logs | 90 days |
| Legal correspondence | 7 years, as required for legal record-keeping |

5.2 When personal data is no longer required and its retention period has expired, it will be securely and permanently deleted or anonymised in a manner that means it can no longer be linked to any individual.
5.3 In some cases, we may need to retain data for longer than the periods stated above — for example, if it is the subject of an ongoing legal dispute or regulatory investigation. In such cases, we will notify you where possible and practicable.

SECTION 6: WHO WE SHARE YOUR DATA WITH
6.1 Our Core Principle: Free-Fall will never sell, rent, trade, or otherwise commercially transfer your personal data to any third party under any circumstances, for any price, at any time. This is an absolute and unconditional commitment.
6.2 Limited Sharing for Operational Purposes: In certain limited circumstances, Free-Fall may share personal data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform. These providers are:
Email Service Providers: To deliver our newsletter and manage subscriber lists. These providers are contractually prohibited from using subscriber data for any purpose other than delivering our newsletter.
Web Hosting and Infrastructure Providers: To host and maintain our website and associated systems.
Analytics Providers: To provide us with anonymised, aggregated data about platform usage. We ensure that no personally identifiable information is shared with analytics providers.
Cybersecurity Providers: To protect our platform against security threats and unauthorised access.
6.3 Requirements for Third-Party Providers: Before engaging any third-party service provider that may have access to personal data, Free-Fall requires them to:
Enter into a written data processing agreement with us
Confirm that they comply with applicable data protection law
Agree to process personal data only for the specific, agreed purpose
Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures
Delete or return all personal data upon termination of the relationship
6.4 Disclosure Required by Law: Free-Fall may disclose personal data to law enforcement authorities, courts, or regulatory bodies if required to do so by applicable law, a valid court order, or a legitimate and properly authorised legal process. In all cases, we will disclose only the minimum data necessary to comply with the legal requirement and will, where legally permissible, notify affected users promptly.
6.5 Disclosure to Protect Safety: In exceptional circumstances, Free-Fall may disclose personal data to relevant authorities if we have a good-faith belief that doing so is necessary to prevent imminent harm to a person's life, safety, or physical wellbeing — for example, if a user communicates information suggesting they or someone else is in immediate danger.
6.6 What We Will NEVER Do:
Share your data with advertisers or marketing companies
Sell your data to data brokers or commercial data aggregators
Share your data with political organisations or campaigns
Share your data with any government outside of a lawful legal process
Use your data to build advertising profiles or targeted marketing lists

SECTION 8: YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA SUBJECT
8.1 Under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance of Hong Kong, and consistent with international best practices, you have the following rights in relation to the personal data we hold about you:

🔍 Right of Access You have the right to request a copy of all personal data we hold about you. We will provide this information free of charge within 40 calendar days of receiving a valid request. We may ask you to verify your identity before fulfilling an access request, to protect against unauthorised disclosure.

✏️ Right to Correction If you believe that any personal data we hold about you is inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated, you have the right to request that we correct it. We will act on all valid correction requests within 40 calendar days.

🗑️ Right to Erasure You have the right to request that we delete personal data we hold about you, subject to certain exceptions — for example, where we are required to retain the data by law, or where the data is necessary for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

🚫 Right to Object You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data where we rely on legitimate interests as our legal basis. We will stop processing the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.

📭 Right to Withdraw Consent Where we process your personal data on the basis of your consent — for example, for our newsletter — you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

📦 Right to Data Portability Where technically feasible, you have the right to receive a copy of the personal data you have provided to us in a commonly used, machine-readable format, and to transmit it to another organisation.

⚖️ Right to Lodge a Complaint If you believe that Free-Fall has handled your personal data unlawfully or in breach of this Privacy Policy, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD) in Hong Kong:
Website: www.pcpd.org.hk
Hotline: +852 2827 2827
Email: complaints@pcpd.org.hk
We would always appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns directly before you approach the regulator, so we encourage you to contact us first at social.freefall@gmail.com.

8.2 How to Exercise Your Rights: To exercise any of the rights described above, please send a written request to social.freefall@gmail.com. Please include your full name, email address, the specific right you wish to exercise, and any relevant details to help us locate your data. We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and respond fully within 40 calendar days.
8.3 Identity Verification: To protect your privacy, we may need to verify your identity before fulfilling any data rights request. We will ask for the minimum information necessary to confirm your identity and will not use this verification information for any other purpose.

SECTION 9: CHILDREN'S PRIVACY — SPECIAL PROTECTIONS
9.1 Our Commitment to Young Readers: Free-Fall is proud that its readership includes young people as young as 5 years old. This is a responsibility we take with the utmost seriousness. We have designed our privacy practices with the safety and wellbeing of young readers as our highest priority.
9.2 Age Threshold: Free-Fall does not knowingly collect, store, or process personal data from children under the age of 13 without the verified consent of a parent or legal guardian. Our newsletter subscription page and submission forms require users to confirm that they are 13 or older, or that they have parental consent.
9.3 Parental Rights: Parents and legal guardians have the right to:
Request access to any personal data we hold about their child
Request correction or deletion of any personal data we hold about their child
Withdraw consent to our processing of their child's personal data at any time
Prohibit us from contacting their child directly
To exercise any of these rights, please contact social.freefall@gmail.com.
9.4 Accidental Collection: If Free-Fall becomes aware that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under the age of 13 without proper parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that data securely. If you are a parent or guardian who believes this has occurred, please contact us immediately at social.freefall@gmail.com.
9.5 Parental Guidance: We strongly encourage parents and guardians to engage actively with their children's use of news and information platforms, including Free-Fall. We welcome parental enquiries, feedback, and involvement at all times.
9.6 No Behavioural Advertising to Minors: Free-Fall will never use data about minor users for behavioural advertising purposes, and we will never knowingly allow any third party to do so through our platform.

SECTION 10: DATA SECURITY
10.1 Our Security Commitment: Free-Fall takes the security of your personal data extremely seriously. We implement a range of appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure, or access.
10.2 Security Measures We Employ Include:
Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our website is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols, indicated by the padlock icon in your browser address bar
Access Controls: Access to personal data within our organisation is strictly limited to those team members who genuinely need it to perform their specific role. All such individuals are trained in data protection obligations
Secure Storage: Personal data is stored on secure servers with appropriate access restrictions and regular security audits
Regular Security Reviews: We periodically review and update our security practices to address new and emerging threats
Incident Response: We maintain a documented data breach response plan to enable us to respond quickly and effectively to any security incidents
10.3 Limitations: No electronic system is entirely immune to security risks. While we take every reasonable precaution, Free-Fall cannot guarantee the absolute security of data transmitted over the internet or stored on electronic systems. We ask users to play their part by using strong passwords, keeping their devices secure, and contacting us immediately if they suspect any unauthorised use of their personal data.
10.4 Data Breach Notification: In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, Free-Fall will notify affected users and the relevant regulatory authority as soon as practicable after becoming aware of the breach, in accordance with our obligations under applicable law.
